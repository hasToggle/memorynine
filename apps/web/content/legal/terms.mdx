## 2. What the service does

memorynine takes what your team captures after client conversations — voice
memos, typed notes, forwarded email — transcribes and reads it, drafts the
people, companies, engagements and facts it finds, and puts those drafts in
front of one of your people to confirm or discard. What gets confirmed becomes
a knowledge base your team can question in plain language, with each answer
showing the material it rests on.

## 5. Recording is your call, and your responsibility

This is the most important clause in this document, so it is not at the bottom.

memorynine is designed around a memo you record **about** a conversation, after
it, in your own voice. That is a different thing from recording the
conversation itself, and the law treats it very differently.

- In Germany, recording somebody's spoken word without their consent can be a
  criminal offence under § 201 StGB. It is not a grey area, and it does not
  become one because the recording was useful.
- Elsewhere in the EU the rules differ by member state, and some require every
  participant's consent.
- Whether you may capture what a client told you, and whether you must tell
  them you are doing it, is a question about your relationship with them. It is
  not a question we can answer for you.

So: **you decide what goes into your workspace, and you carry the legal
responsibility for it.** You confirm that you have a lawful basis for the
personal data you put in, that you have met your information duties under
Art. 13 and 14 GDPR toward the people it describes, and that you are not
uploading recordings you had no right to make.

We will not police this for you, and we would be doing you a disservice if we
implied that using our product settled it.

For everything inside your workspace, your company is the controller and we are

The [privacy notice](/legal/privacy) sets out where data sits, which

Do not deliberately put special categories of data (Art. 9 GDPR) into a
workspace — health, beliefs, union membership, and the rest. The transcription
step strips the medical and financial categories it can recognise, but that is
data minimisation, not a filter you should rely on.

## 8. What the assistant says is not advice

The assistant drafts, cites and answers. It does not decide.

It is built to say when it does not know, to show both sides of a
contradiction, and to mark material nobody has confirmed. Those are real
design commitments and they are tested. They are not a warranty that any given
answer is correct.

Every answer carries the material it rests on precisely so a person can check
it. What your team repeats to a client is your team's word, not ours.
